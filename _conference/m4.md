## Scope

This collection examines how adapting antenna positions, shapes, and radiation patterns can help wireless links cope with fading and interference. The selected readings connect the fundamentals of reconfigurable antennas with practical designs for reliable multiuser communications.

### Fundamentals, Channel Modeling, and Estimation

- Surveys and tutorials on reconfigurable antennas and fluid antenna systems
- Theoretical analysis of outage probability and spatial multiplexing
- Channel modeling, estimation, and prediction for reconfigurable antennas

### Enabling Technologies and Architectures

- Fluid antenna systems, movable antennas, and reconfigurable pixel antennas
- Reconfigurable massive MIMO and tri-hybrid MIMO architectures
- Antenna configuration, signal processing, and learning-based control

### Reliable Transmission and Applications

- Robust waveform design, beamforming, and interference mitigation
- Physical-layer security and anti-jamming techniques
- Applications in dense indoor environments, UAV networks, and satellite systems
