## Scope

Ubiquitous communication systems aim to integrate space, air, ground, and maritime networks to provide seamless global connectivity and support emerging communication services. The growing demand for immersive applications, autonomous systems, and digital twins presents new challenges in network architecture, transmission efficiency, intelligent resource management, and communication security.

The conference will bring together researchers, engineers, and industry experts to explore fundamental theories, innovative methodologies, network architectures, and practical solutions for future ubiquitous communication systems.

### Communication Theory and Advanced Signal Processing

- Channel estimation, propagation modeling, and information theory
- Next-generation MIMO, reconfigurable surfaces, and millimeter-wave and terahertz communications
- Physical-layer security, modulation, and channel coding
- Advanced signal processing and image processing techniques

### Networking and Optical Communications

- Network architecture, resource management, and spectrum sharing
- Ultra-reliable low-latency communications and network security
- Edge computing and energy-efficient wireless networking
- Optical transmission systems, optical wireless communications, and photonic technologies

### Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Communication Technologies

- Machine learning and intelligent optimization for wireless systems
- Semantic communications and AI-enabled network management
- Integrated sensing, computing, and communications
- Non-terrestrial networks, satellite communications, and emerging 6G technologies
