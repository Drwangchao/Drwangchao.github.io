## Scope

Non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) can extend connectivity beyond the reach of terrestrial infrastructure and support services such as emergency communications. Their broadcast links, long propagation delays, changing network topologies, and limited onboard resources present challenges for secure and reliable communications.

The workshop will examine communication theory, system design, and optimization methods for addressing these challenges. It will provide a forum for researchers and engineers from academia and industry to discuss advances in transmission, network operation, and emerging technologies for 6G NTNs.

### Physical-Layer Security and Privacy

- Physical-layer security and privacy-preserving transmission
- Secure multiple access and covert communications
- Secure semantic communications and integrated covert communication and sensing

### Reliable Transmission and Reconfigurable Antennas

- Waveform design, beamforming, and precoding for reliable NTN links
- Finite-blocklength transmission under latency and reliability constraints
- Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and fluid antenna systems

### AI-Assisted Network Operation

- Learning-assisted mobility management and digital twins
- Trustworthy AI, secure AI-enabled radio access networks, and multi-agent coordination
- Secure computation offloading and distributed computing
