## Scope

Industrial private beyond-5G networks provide high-capacity connectivity, low-latency transmission, and reliable wireless access for industrial automation. Private networking solutions are particularly important in factories and remote industrial environments where public mobile networks may not meet demanding coverage and performance requirements.

The workshop brought together researchers and engineers from academia and industry to discuss theoretical advances and practical solutions for industrial private wireless networks, with particular emphasis on intelligent network management, advanced transmission technologies, and secure industrial applications.

### Private Network Architectures and Intelligent Resource Management

- Open RAN and energy-efficient private network deployment
- Integration with existing industrial communication infrastructure
- Intelligent network orchestration, edge computing, and radio resource optimization
- Traffic forecasting and privacy-preserving distributed learning

### Reliable Transmission and Advanced Wireless Technologies

- Multi-band aggregation, multi-link transmission, and distributed MIMO
- Enhanced ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC)
- Interference mitigation and adaptive radio hardware
- Intelligent reflecting surfaces for industrial private networks

### Industrial Applications, Security, and Emerging Technologies

- Time-sensitive networking (TSN) for industrial communications
- Satellite- and UAV-assisted non-terrestrial connectivity
- High-precision positioning and integrated sensing and communications (ISAC)
- Secure low-latency transmission and smart grid security
