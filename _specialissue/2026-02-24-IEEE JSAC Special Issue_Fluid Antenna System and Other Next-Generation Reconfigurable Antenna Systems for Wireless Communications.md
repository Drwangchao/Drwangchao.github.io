## Scope

Fluid antenna systems (FAS) and other next-generation reconfigurable antenna (NGRA) systems introduce additional spatial degrees of freedom through dynamically adaptable antenna structures. These technologies offer opportunities to improve diversity, multiplexing, and interference management in future wireless networks.

This special issue explores theoretical foundations, technological innovations, and practical applications of FAS and NGRA systems for 6G communications, including their integration with emerging wireless technologies, intelligent system optimization, and hardware implementations.

### Topics of Interest

- Physics-, antenna-, and circuit-based system modeling
- Electromagnetic and information-theoretic performance limits
- Advanced optimization methods
- AI-assisted algorithms, management, and protocols
- Channel estimation, extrapolation, and reconstruction
- Coding and modulation
- Multiple access for massive connectivity
- Interference mitigation in cell-free and multicell networks
- Millimeter-wave and terahertz communications
- Integration with reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS)
- Joint communications, sensing, and computing
- Physical-layer security and privacy
- Connections with holographic MIMO
- V2X and non-terrestrial networks (NTNs)
- Energy efficiency, energy harvesting, and wireless power transfer
- Industrial applications, experimental trials, and testbeds

For the original scope and detailed research topics, please refer to the [Official Call for Papers](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-jsac/cfp/fluid-antenna-system-and-other-next-generation-reconfigurable).
