## Scope

Privacy and trust are fundamental requirements for future 6G wireless networks. The increasing deployment of sensing technologies and intelligent monitoring systems presents new security challenges that cannot be fully addressed by conventional encryption and authentication.

This special issue focuses on the theoretical foundations, system design, and applications of intelligent covert communications. It explores the use of artificial intelligence (AI), digital twins (DTs), and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) to improve communication privacy and reliability across terrestrial, non-terrestrial, and hybrid networks.

### Topics of Interest

- Covert communications over non-terrestrial networks (NTNs)
- Intelligent detection and counter-detection methods
- Tradeoffs between communication efficiency and covertness
- Channel characterization and uncertainty analysis
- Reinforcement learning and game theory for covert communications
- Digital twins for intelligent covert systems
- ISAC-assisted covert communications
- Semantic communications for covert information representation
- Covertness-constrained resource optimization and beamforming
- Covert communications using massive MIMO
- RIS-assisted covert communications
- Covert communications over THz and mmWave bands
- Federated learning supported by covert communications
- LLM-assisted covert communications
- Covert communications in AI-enabled Internet of Things (AIoT)
- Cross-layer architectures and protocol optimization
- Experimental platforms for covert communications
- Practical datasets for covert communication research

For the original scope and detailed research topics, please refer to the [Official Call for Papers](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-ojcoms/cfp/intelligent-covert-communications-next-generation-wireless).
