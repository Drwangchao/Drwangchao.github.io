## Scope

The integration of communications, sensing, computing, and native intelligence in 6G networks introduces increasingly complex security challenges. Emerging technologies, including agentic AI and quantum computing, further highlight the limitations of conventional security mechanisms.

Wireless endogenous security (WES) incorporates security directly into wireless system architectures through dynamic-heterogeneous-redundancy (DHR) and intelligent cross-layer coordination.

This special issue focuses on WES theories, methodologies, and applications for resilient, adaptive, and intrinsically secure next-generation wireless networks.

### Topics of Interest

- Threat modeling for WES
- WES-based wiretap coding for secure 6G networks
- Native AI for security orchestration
- WES-enabled RSMA, NOMA, and advanced multiple access
- Physical-layer security in wireless systems
- Resilient AI agents and autonomous recovery
- WES-based zero-trust authentication
- WES for ISAC, space-air-ground-sea integrated networks, and the Internet of Everything
- Secure federated learning through WES
- WES for massive MIMO, extremely large MIMO, and holographic MIMO
- WES for mmWave, THz, semantic communications, and over-the-air computing
- Sustainable security design balancing QoS/QoE and energy efficiency
- FAS-assisted wireless endogenous security
- RIS-assisted wireless endogenous security
- Quantum-resilient communications using WES
- Standardization of WES for future networks
- Experimental validation, testbeds, and case studies

For the original scope and detailed research topics, please refer to the [Official Call for Papers](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/magazines/ieee-wireless-communications/cfp/wireless-endogenous-security-ai-native-next-generation-wireless-networks).
