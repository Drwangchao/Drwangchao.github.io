**1.	Title**

**Intelligent and Secure Communications in Non-Terrestrial Networks**


**2.	Background, Motivation, and Technical Scope of the Proposed Special Issue**

The evolution toward 6G-enabled global connectivity is driving an unprecedented integration of non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) into future wireless infrastructures. While NTNs are essential for ubiquitous coverage, emergency response, and seamless connectivity, their broadcast nature, long propagation distances, highly dynamic topologies, and limited onboard resources introduce fundamentally new security, privacy, and reliability challenges that are not adequately addressed by existing terrestrial communication paradigms.

At the same time, emerging applications such as tactical IoT, emergency communications, intelligent transportation, and military–civil fusion systems require not only conventional cryptographic security, but also low-probability-of-detection (LPD), low-probability-of-intercept (LPI), and covert communication capabilities, especially in contested or adversarial environments. These requirements call for cross-layer, intelligence-driven, and physics-aware communication designs that go beyond traditional security mechanisms.

Recent advances in artificial intelligence, learning-based signal processing, semantic communications, and reconfigurable radio technologies offer powerful new tools to enable intelligent, adaptive, and proactive secure/covert communication strategies in NTNs. However, the integration of intelligence with physical-layer security, covert communication theory, and NTN-specific channel characteristics remains an open and timely research challenge. Issues such as AI-assisted attack detection, learning-based beamforming for covertness, secured integrated sensing and communication design, and resilience against intelligent adversaries are still in their infancy and lack a dedicated forum.

The main GLOBECOM symposia primarily focus on general wireless communications, networking, and security techniques, with limited emphasis on the unique interplay among intelligence, security, covertness, and non-terrestrial propagation environments. In contrast, this workshop aims to provide a focused and interdisciplinary platform that brings together researchers and practitioners from NTN communications, physical-layer security, covert communications, and AI-enabled wireless systems, fostering discussions on emerging threats, novel theoretical frameworks, and practical system designs tailored specifically for 6G non-terrestrial and integrated ground–air–space networks.

• By addressing intelligent, secure, and covert communication paradigms in NTNs—topics not explicitly covered by existing GLOBECOM workshops or symposia—the proposed workshop is highly timely and directly aligned with the vision of resilient, trustworthy, and intelligent 6G global connectivity.
